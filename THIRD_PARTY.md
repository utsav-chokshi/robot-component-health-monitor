# Dataset and dependency notices

The selected source is the IMS bearing dataset produced by the NSF I/UCR Center for Intelligent Maintenance Systems, University of Cincinnati, with Rexnord support, distributed through NASA's Prognostics Center of Excellence repository.

NASA lists J. Lee, H. Qiu, G. Yu, J. Lin and Rexnord Technical Services (2007), Bearing Data Set, and requests acknowledgment of the repository and contributors. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/ .

The accepted experiment is an industrial bearing test rig, not robot telemetry. Raw archives, the original PDF and model weights are not bundled or relicensed by this application's MIT license. Preparation requires an explicit source download, checksum verification and manual archive extraction; ingestion and replay never download data. Derived configuration, filenames/hashes and project-authored summaries do not constitute redistribution of the original raw recordings.

Python dependencies and container software retain their respective licenses. The rejected optional model pilot was external feasibility work and no model runtime/dependency or weights are included in this foundation.
